Good to know before booking a flight to Ouagadougou

Flights to Ouagadougou leave from 9 cities: Abidjan, Accra, Lagos, Lomé, Dakar, Casablanca, Addis Ababa, Brussels, Paris.

The shortest flight is from Abidjan (about 1 h 30); the longest from Addis Ababa (about 6 h).

Prices are highest during July–August and the year-end holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare direct flights and flights with a stop: on this route, a well-placed connection often lowers the price. Also check the baggage allowance, often decisive for family trips.
  • Direct flight options vary with the situation: trips connecting via Abidjan, Casablanca, Algiers or Istanbul are common.
