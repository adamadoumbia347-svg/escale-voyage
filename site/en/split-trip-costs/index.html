      How does it work?

      1. Add each traveller's first name and choose the currency.
      2. Enter each expense: what it was, the amount, who paid and for whom. Someone was not involved? Untick them.
      3. Escale works out everyone's share and the shortest possible list of transfers.
      4. Send the result on WhatsApp: the link opens the same tally for the other travellers.

      Frequently asked questions

      Where are my expenses stored?

      Only on your phone or computer. Nothing is sent to Escale and there is no account to create. If you clear your browser data, the tally is erased.

      What does the sharing link contain?

      The tally itself: the names, the expenses and the currency. Anyone who receives the link can therefore read them. If someone adds an expense on their side, they need to send their link again for the others to see it.

      How are the transfers calculated?

      Escale adds up what each person paid, subtracts their share of each expense, then has those who owe money pay those who advanced it, starting with the largest amounts. Everyone gets their money back with few transfers.

      What if expenses are in several currencies?

      A tally uses a single currency. Convert the amounts yourself before entering them, for example at the rate your card actually charged.

      An expense only concerns part of the group?

      Untick the people who are not involved under “For whom?”: the expense is split among the others only.
