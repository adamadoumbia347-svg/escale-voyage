Multi-city flights

London → Lagos → Accra → London? Enter your legs: Escale prepares one search per leg, with the lowest price spotted that month when it is known.

Your legs

How it works

  • Each leg is a one-way flight: you compare and book the tickets one by one.
  • The arrival of one leg becomes the departure of the next, to save time.
  • The price shown is the lowest recently spotted for that month, per person: it is confirmed when you open the search.

Before you book

  • Separate tickets = unprotected connection. If one flight is late, the next one does not wait and is not refunded. Leave a wide margin, ideally a night.
  • You have to collect and re-check your bags at each leg.
  • Each country has its own entry rules: check with the visa tool.
  • Always compare with a plain return: two returns from the same city are sometimes cheaper. Compare a return flight.

Frequently asked questions

Why one search per leg?

Because separate tickets are often the cheapest way to visit two countries in one trip, and each leg can then be compared like a one-way flight. The tool prepares those searches for you, with the right dates.

What is the risk with separate tickets?

The flights are not linked. If the first is late and you miss the next, the second airline owes you nothing. Leave a wide margin between two legs, ideally a night, and collect your bags at each leg.

Do I need a visa for each country?

Yes, every country you enter applies its own rules, even for a short stop. Check before booking with Escale's visa tool and on the official sites.

What about a single ticket with a long stopover?

Some airlines let you stay one or more days in their hub on a single ticket. That is safer than two separate tickets. Look at the offers of the airline linking your two cities.
