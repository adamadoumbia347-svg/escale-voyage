    Frequently asked questions

    How long is a flight from Dakar to Kano?

    About 4 h non-stop (estimate based on the 2,780 km distance). With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Dakar to Kano?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and the year-end holidays: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Kano?

    Flights arrive at Mallam Aminu International Airport (KAN).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
