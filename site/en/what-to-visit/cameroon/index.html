What to visit in Cameroon 🇨🇲

"Africa in miniature": Atlantic beaches, a volcano, the chiefdoms of the West and the savannahs of the North. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Olivier Charly1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

DoualaYaoundéKribiLimbeMount CameroonFoumbanRhumsikiWaza National Park
City1

Douala

The economic capital and the country's main port, on the Wouri estuary. Pictured here, the Bali district.

Photo : Olivier Charly1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital2

Yaoundé

The political capital, built on hills. Pictured here, the Immeuble Étoile, in the administrative district.

Photo : Ryanvanhuyssteen, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach3

Kribi

The seaside resort of the south: sandy beaches and the Lobé Falls, which drop straight into the ocean.

Photo : Blaizo 237, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach4

Limbe

At the foot of Mount Cameroon, beaches of black volcanic sand.

Photo : Blaizo 237, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Volcano5

Mount Cameroon

An active volcano of more than 4,000 m, the highest peak in West and Central Africa.

Photo : Adesolive, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Culture6

Foumban

The capital of the Bamum kingdom, in the West: the Sultans' Palace and its craftspeople.

Photo : Elin, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery7

Rhumsiki

In the Far North, a village surrounded by volcanic peaks, in the heart of the Mandara Mountains.

Photo : 2ddanga, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Safari8

Waza National Park

In the Far North, a large savannah reserve: elephants, giraffes, antelopes.

Photo : Albert Backer, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
