What to visit in Cameroon 🇨🇲

The great port of Douala, the capital on its hills, the beaches of Kribi and the palace of the Bamum sultans. 4 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Olivier Charly1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

DoualaYaoundéKribiFoumban
City1

Douala

The economic capital and the country's main port, on the Wouri estuary. Pictured here, the Bali district.

Photo : Olivier Charly1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital2

Yaoundé

The political capital, built on hills. Pictured here, the Immeuble Étoile, in the administrative district.

Photo : Ryanvanhuyssteen, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach3

Kribi

The seaside resort of the south: sandy beaches and the Lobé Falls, which drop straight into the ocean.

Photo : Blaizo 237, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Culture4

Foumban

The capital of the Bamum kingdom, in the West: the Sultans' Palace and its craftspeople.

Photo : Elin, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons
