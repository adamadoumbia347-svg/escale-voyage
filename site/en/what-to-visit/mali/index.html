What to visit in Mali 🇲🇱

The Niger River, great earthen mosques, villages clinging to a cliff and sandstone needles. 6 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Carolinerre, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

BamakoDjennéTimbuktuDogon CountryMoptiHombori
Capital1

Bamako

The capital spreads over both banks of the Niger River, still crossed by pirogues.

Photo : Carolinerre, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage2

Djenné

The Great Mosque, the largest mud-brick building in the world. The old towns of Djenné have been listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Ruud Zwart, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town3

Timbuktu

On the edge of the Sahara, the city of earthen mosques and ancient manuscripts. Listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Anne and David, Public domain, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery4

Dogon Country

Villages and granaries built at the foot of, and into, the Bandiagara Escarpment. Listed by UNESCO since 1989.

Photo : Kirua, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

River5

Mopti

A large river port at the confluence of the Niger and the Bani, where the pinasses (river boats) moor.

Photo : Jeanne Menjoulet, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery6

Hombori

Sandstone needles rising above the plain: the Hand of Fatima, near Mount Hombori.

Photo : Timm Guenther ( Timm Busshaus ), CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
