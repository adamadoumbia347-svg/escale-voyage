What to visit in the Netherlands 🇳🇱

Canals, windmills, tulips and bicycles: Holland, and all the rest of the country. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

AmsterdamRijksmuseumKinderdijkLisseKeukenhofGiethoornRotterdamThe Hague
Capital1

Amsterdam

Narrow houses along the water. The 17th-century canals have been listed by UNESCO since 2010.

Photo : Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Museum2

Rijksmuseum

Amsterdam's great museum, where you can see Rembrandt's The Night Watch.

Photo : Trougnouf (Benoit Brummer), CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage3

Kinderdijk

19 windmills lined up along the water, listed by UNESCO since 1997.

Photo : Dmytro Balkhovitin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature4

Lisse

In spring, the tulip fields form bands of colour as far as the eye can see.

Photo : Michielverbeek, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Garden5

Keukenhof

A vast flower park, open only in spring.

Photo : Willem van Valk, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Village6

Giethoorn

Thatched-roof houses and canals: here, people get around by small boat.

Photo : KarelJanda, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City7

Rotterdam

The country's main port, with modern architecture: the yellow Cube Houses are tilted onto one corner.

Photo : P. Hughes, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City8

The Hague

The seat of government: the Binnenhof, a group of old buildings beside a pond.

Photo : Michielverbeek, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
