What to visit in Mexico 🇲🇽

Pyramids, turquoise sea, colonial cities and legendary cuisine. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Fcb981, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Chichén ItzáTeotihuacanTulumMexico CityOaxacaGuanajuatoCancúnCenotes
Ancient site1

Chichén Itzá

The pyramid of Kukulcán, in the heart of the Yucatán. A Maya site listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Fcb981, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site2

Teotihuacan

The Pyramid of the Sun, near Mexico City. A site listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside3

Tulum

Maya ruins set on the cliff, above a beach on the Caribbean Sea.

Photo : Erik Cleves Kristensen, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital4

Mexico City

The Palace of Fine Arts, inaugurated in 1934, at the heart of a vast city.

Photo : Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

City5

Oaxaca

The church of Santo Domingo and a historic centre listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : Radiodreamer, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City6

Guanajuato

A mining town with houses of every colour, clinging to the hills. Listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Yamen, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach7

Cancún

On the Caribbean Sea: a long strip of white sand and turquoise water.

Photo : dronepicr, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature8

Cenotes

In the Yucatán, natural freshwater wells hollowed out of the rock, where you go down to swim.

Photo : Shinya Suzuki, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons
