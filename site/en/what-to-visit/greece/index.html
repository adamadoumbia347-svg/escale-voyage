What to visit in Greece 🇬🇷

Ancient temples, perched monasteries and white islands above the sea. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : A.Savin, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

AthensSantoriniMykonosMeteoraDelphiCreteRhodesZakynthos
Capital1

Athens

The Acropolis and the Parthenon, from the 5th century BC. Listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : A.Savin, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island2

Santorini

White villages with blue domes, clinging to the rim of an ancient volcano. Pictured here, Oia.

Photo : Giles Laurent, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island3

Mykonos

In the Cyclades, an island known for its windmills and its white lanes.

Photo : Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Monasteries4

Meteora

Monasteries built on top of rock pinnacles. Listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site5

Delphi

The sanctuary where people came to consult the oracle. Listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island6

Crete

The largest Greek island. Pictured here, the Balos lagoon, in the north-west.

Photo : Mwchalmers, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Medieval town7

Rhodes

An old town fortified by the Knights of St John. Listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Vadim Indeikin, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach8

Zakynthos

Navagio beach, at the foot of the cliffs, which can be seen from above or from the sea.

Photo : kallerna, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
