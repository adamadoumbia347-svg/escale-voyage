What to visit in Guinea 🇬🇳

A capital on the ocean, islands just offshore, and the Fouta Djallon plateaus with their waterfalls. 7 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Boubacar Bila Diao Balde, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

ConakryLoos IslandsFouta DjallonDalabaKindiaKambadaga FallsDitinn Falls
Capital1

Conakry

The capital stretches into the Atlantic along a narrow peninsula.

Photo : Boubacar Bila Diao Balde, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Islands2

Loos Islands

A small archipelago off Conakry, reached by boat: beaches and fishing villages.

Photo : Falilou224, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain3

Fouta Djallon

A highland of green plateaus and cliffs, where several of West Africa's great rivers rise, including the Senegal and the Gambia.

Photo : Maarten van der Bent, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Dalaba

A small hill town in the Fouta Djallon. Pictured here, its mosque.

Photo : Aboubacarkhoraa, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Waterfall5

Kindia

Near the town, the Bridal Veil waterfall drops in a fine spray down the rock face.

Photo : M keita1321, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Waterfall6

Kambadaga Falls

In the Fouta Djallon, a series of falls on several levels, in the middle of the forest.

Photo : Flucco, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Waterfall7

Ditinn Falls

Near Dalaba, a tall waterfall dropping from a cliff.

Photo : Aboubacarkhoraa, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
