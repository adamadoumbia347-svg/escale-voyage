What to visit in Brazil 🇧🇷

Rio and its beaches, the Iguazu Falls, the Amazon and dunes dotted with lagoons. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Donatas Dabravolskas, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Rio de JaneiroSugarloaf MountainCopacabanaIguazu FallsSalvadorThe AmazonLençóis MaranhensesSão Paulo
City1

Rio de Janeiro

Christ the Redeemer, inaugurated in 1931 at the top of Corcovado, arms open over the bay.

Photo : Donatas Dabravolskas, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery2

Sugarloaf Mountain

In Rio, a granite peak at the entrance to the bay, which you reach by cable car.

Photo : Donatas Dabravolskas, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach3

Copacabana

In Rio, a four-kilometre beach lined by a promenade of black and white paving stones.

Photo : Donatas Dabravolskas, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature4

Iguazu Falls

On the border with Argentina, hundreds of waterfalls in the middle of the forest. Listed by UNESCO since 1986.

Photo : Acediscovery, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town5

Salvador

The Pelourinho, the district of colourful houses in Brazil's first capital. Listed by UNESCO since 1985.

Photo : Paul R. Burley, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature6

The Amazon

The largest river in the world by discharge, and the forest that surrounds it. Pictured here, the forest near Manaus, on the banks of the Rio Negro.

Photo : James Martins, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery7

Lençóis Maranhenses

White dunes dotted with rainwater lagoons. Listed by UNESCO since 2024.

Photo : Paulo Cattelan, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City8

São Paulo

The largest city in the country and in South America. Pictured here, Ibirapuera Park and, behind it, the towers.

Photo : Sérgio Valle Duarte, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
