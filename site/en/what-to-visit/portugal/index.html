What to visit in Portugal 🇵🇹

Yellow trams, colourful palaces, golden cliffs and terraced vineyards. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Romazur, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

LisbonBelémSintraPortoDouro ValleyAlgarveÓbidosMadeira
Capital1

Lisbon

Hills, narrow lanes and tram 28, which runs through the old quarters.

Photo : Romazur, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Belém

In Lisbon, the Belém Tower, built in the 16th century on the banks of the Tagus. Listed by UNESCO since 1983.

Photo : Norbert Nagel, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Palace3

Sintra

The brightly coloured Pena Palace, on a wooded hill. Listed by UNESCO since 1995.

Photo : CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Porto

The Ribeira district, on the banks of the Douro, and the Dom Luís I Bridge. Centre listed by UNESCO since 1996.

Photo : Krzysztof Golik, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery5

Douro Valley

Terraced vineyards along the river. Listed by UNESCO since 2001.

Photo : Marco Varisco, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside6

Algarve

In the south, golden cliffs, arches and caves: pictured here, the Ponta da Piedade, in Lagos.

Photo : Dronepicr, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Village7

Óbidos

A medieval village of white houses, surrounded by ramparts and overlooked by its castle.

Photo : Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island8

Madeira

An Atlantic island, mountainous and green. Pictured here, the heights of Funchal, its capital.

Photo : Ximonic (Simo Räsänen), CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
