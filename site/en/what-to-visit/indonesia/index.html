What to visit in Indonesia 🇮🇩

Bali and its temples, but also the volcanoes of Java, Borobudur and the Komodo dragons. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

BaliUbudTanah LotNusa PenidaBorobudurMount BromoKomodoJakarta
Island1

Bali

The temple of Ulun Danu Bratan, set on a mountain lake. It appears on the 50,000 rupiah banknote.

Photo : CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature2

Ubud

Around Ubud, the rice terraces of Tegallalang. The Balinese irrigation system, the subak, has been listed by UNESCO since 2012.

Photo : Vyacheslav Argenberg, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Temple3

Tanah Lot

In Bali, a temple on a rock in the sea, surrounded by the waves at high tide.

Photo : CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island4

Nusa Penida

Kelingking beach and its cliff, on the island facing Bali.

Photo : Chainwit., CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Temple5

Borobudur

On the island of Java, the largest Buddhist temple in the world, built in the 9th century. Listed by UNESCO since 1991.

Photo : CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Volcano6

Mount Bromo

In the east of Java, an active volcano in the middle of a sea of sand: people climb it for the sunrise.

Photo : Thomas Hirsch, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature7

Komodo

A national park of arid islands, refuge of the Komodo dragon, the largest lizard in the world. Listed by UNESCO since 1991. Pictured here, Padar Island.

Photo : Jakub Hałun, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City8

Jakarta

The country's largest city, on the island of Java, and its National Monument (Monas).

Photo : Gunawan Kartapranata, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
