What to visit in Gabon 🇬🇦

A capital on the ocean, and parks where the equatorial forest meets savannah and beach. 6 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Shirmy25, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

LibrevillePointe-DenisLopé National ParkLoango National ParkLambarénéIvindo National Park
Capital1

Libreville

The capital, on the Gabon estuary, and its long seafront.

Photo : Shirmy25, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach2

Pointe-Denis

A sandy peninsula opposite Libreville, reached by boat.

Photo : Nolween, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature3

Lopé National Park

A landscape of savannah and forest in the centre of the country, home to forest elephants. Listed by UNESCO since 2007.

Photo : Ngangorica, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature4

Loango National Park

On the coast, a park where forest, savannah and lagoons meet the ocean: elephants can be seen right down near the beach.

Photo : Kurt Dundy, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

History5

Lambaréné

On the banks of the Ogooué River, the hospital founded by Dr Albert Schweitzer, winner of the 1952 Nobel Peace Prize.

Photo : Patrice H, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Forest6

Ivindo National Park

A vast equatorial forest. Pictured here, the Langoué clearing, where elephants gather. Listed by UNESCO since 2021.

Photo : Ngangorica, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
