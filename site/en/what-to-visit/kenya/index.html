What to visit in Kenya 🇰🇪

Safaris, flamingos, snow-capped peaks and Indian Ocean beaches. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Maasai MaraAmboseliNairobiLake NakuruMount KenyaLamuDiani BeachMombasa
Safari1

Maasai Mara

The country's most famous reserve, in the south-west: lions, cheetahs, elephants and great herds of wildebeest.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Safari2

Amboseli

Herds of elephants with, as a backdrop, Kilimanjaro, on the other side of the border.

Photo : M. Disdero, CC BY-SA 2.5, Wikimedia Commons

Capital3

Nairobi

A national park on the city's doorstep: you can see giraffes there in front of the skyscrapers.

Photo : Alexmbogo, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature4

Lake Nakuru

A Rift Valley lake known for its flamingos. Kenya's Rift Valley lakes have been listed by UNESCO since 2011.

Photo : Syllabub, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain5

Mount Kenya

5,199 m: the second-highest peak in Africa, listed by UNESCO since 1997.

Photo : Galkey, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town6

Lamu

An old Swahili town on an island, with narrow lanes. Listed by UNESCO since 2001.

Photo : Abubakarsule533, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach7

Diani Beach

White sand and the Indian Ocean, south of Mombasa.

Photo : Bingar1234, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City8

Mombasa

The main port on the coast and its Fort Jesus, built by the Portuguese at the end of the 16th century and listed by UNESCO since 2011.

Photo : Bertina Kanaka, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
