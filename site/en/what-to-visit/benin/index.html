What to visit in Benin 🇧🇯

A village on the water, the palaces of the kings of Abomey, Ouidah and its memory, a capital with Afro-Brazilian façades and beaches. 7 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Adoscam, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

CotonouGanviéOuidahTemple of PythonsAbomeyPorto-NovoGrand-Popo
City1

Cotonou

The economic capital, between the ocean and Lake Nokoué. Pictured here, the Cadjèhoun district.

Photo : Adoscam, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Village2

Ganvié

A lake village on Lake Nokoué: the houses stand on stilts and everything is done by pirogue.

Photo : Dr. Ondřej Havelka (cestovatel), CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Memorial3

Ouidah

At the end of the Slave Route, on the beach, the Door of No Return commemorates the Atlantic slave trade.

Photo : Chiemelacebelae, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Holy city4

Temple of Pythons

In Ouidah, a Vodun temple that is home to pythons, sacred animals.

Photo : Gildaskiki, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage5

Abomey

The red-earth royal palaces of the kings of Dahomey. Listed by UNESCO since 1985.

Photo : Karalyn Monteil, CC BY-SA 3.0 igo, Wikimedia Commons

Capital6

Porto-Novo

The official capital and its Great Mosque, with a colourful Afro-Brazilian-style façade.

Photo : Gildaskiki, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach7

Grand-Popo

A long sandy beach near the mouth of the Mono River, in the west of the country.

Photo : Ji-Elle, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
