What to visit in Italy 🇮🇹

Ancient ruins, cities of art, colourful villages by the sea: each region has its own setting. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Diliff, CC BY-SA 2.5, Wikimedia Commons

RomeVeniceFlorenceMilanCinque TerreAmalfi CoastPisaPompeii
Capital1

Rome

The Colosseum, completed in the year 80, could hold tens of thousands of spectators. The historic centre is listed by UNESCO.

Photo : Diliff, CC BY-SA 2.5, Wikimedia Commons

City2

Venice

A city built on water: no cars here, you get around on foot and by boat. Listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : kallerna, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City of art3

Florence

The cradle of the Renaissance and the dome of its cathedral, completed in the 15th century. Centre listed by UNESCO since 1982.

Photo : Luca Nebuloni, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Milan

The fashion capital and its Duomo, a Gothic marble cathedral that took nearly six centuries to build.

Photo : Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside5

Cinque Terre

Five colourful villages clinging to the cliffs, in Liguria. Listed by UNESCO since 1997.

Photo : Timothy A. Gonsalves, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside6

Amalfi Coast

South of Naples, terraced villages above the sea, such as Positano. Listed by UNESCO since 1997.

Photo : Bernard Gagnon, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark7

Pisa

The Leaning Tower, the cathedral's bell tower, on a square listed by UNESCO since 1987.

Photo : MHoser, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site8

Pompeii

A Roman city buried by the eruption of Vesuvius in 79, and preserved under the ash.

Photo : ElfQrin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
