What to visit in South Africa 🇿🇦

A city between mountain and ocean, safaris, penguins on the beach and canyons. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Cape TownBo-KaapCape of Good HopeBoulders BeachKruger National ParkJohannesburgDrakensbergBlyde River Canyon
City1

Cape Town

The city spreads out at the foot of Table Mountain, a plateau more than 1,000 m high that you reach by cable car.

Photo : Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

District2

Bo-Kaap

In Cape Town, a neighbourhood of houses painted in bright colours, on the slopes of Signal Hill.

Photo : Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery3

Cape of Good Hope

A rocky coast battered by waves and wind, at the tip of the Cape Peninsula.

Photo : Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature4

Boulders Beach

Near Cape Town, a beach that is home to a colony of African penguins.

Photo : Satdeep Gill, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Safari5

Kruger National Park

One of the largest reserves in Africa: lions, elephants, rhinos, buffalo and leopards.

Photo : Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City6

Johannesburg

The country's largest city, born of the gold rush at the end of the 19th century.

Photo : Mark Hillary, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain7

Drakensberg

A range of mountains and cliffs, listed by UNESCO since 2000.

Photo : PhilippN, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery8

Blyde River Canyon

One of the largest canyons in the world, covered in vegetation, and its three rounded peaks.

Photo : Claudirene, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
