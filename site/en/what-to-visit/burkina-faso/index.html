What to visit in Burkina Faso 🇧🇫

An earthen mosque, sandstone peaks carved by erosion, waterfalls and painted houses. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : The black photographer, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

OuagadougouBobo-DioulassoSindou PeaksKarfiguéla FallsDomes of FabédougouTiébéléRuins of LoropéniLaongo
Capital1

Ouagadougou

The capital and its Monument to the National Heroes, in the Ouaga 2000 district.

Photo : The black photographer, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Bobo-Dioulasso

The country's second city and its earthen Great Mosque, bristling with wooden stakes.

Photo : Jurgen, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery3

Sindou Peaks

In the south-west, a chain of sandstone needles carved by erosion.

Photo : Stefan Dressler, CC BY-SA 2.5, Wikimedia Commons

Waterfall4

Karfiguéla Falls

Near Banfora, a river that drops in several falls amid the vegetation.

Photo : Maarten van der Bent, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery5

Domes of Fabédougou

Near Banfora, striped sandstone domes rounded by erosion.

Photo : Marco Schmidt, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Village6

Tiébélé

In the south, near the Ghanaian border, the Kassena royal court and its houses with painted walls in geometric patterns.

Photo : c.hug, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage7

Ruins of Loropéni

High stone walls, the remains of a fortress linked to the gold trade. Listed by UNESCO since 2009.

Photo : Rik Schuiling, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Culture8

Laongo

Near Ouagadougou, an open-air site where artists have carved the granite boulders.

Photo : Phovicom, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
