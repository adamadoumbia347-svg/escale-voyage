What to visit in Morocco 🇲🇦

Medinas, mountains, dunes and ocean: from the imperial cities to the gates of the desert. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Herokk, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

MarrakechChefchaouenFezCasablancaMerzougaEssaouiraAït BenhaddouRabat
City1

Marrakech

Jemaa el-Fna square comes alive every evening: storytellers, musicians, stalls. The medina has been listed by UNESCO since 1985.

Photo : Herokk, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City2

Chefchaouen

In the Rif, a small town whose lanes are painted blue, from the ground up to the doors.

Photo : Mark Fischer, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Imperial city3

Fez

One of the largest medinas in the world, listed by UNESCO since 1981, and its open-air tanneries.

Photo : Uwe Brodrecht, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Casablanca

The economic capital and its Hassan II Mosque, completed in 1993 on the ocean's edge: its minaret is 210 m tall.

Photo : Milamber's portfolio, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Desert5

Merzouga

At the gates of the Sahara, the great dunes of Erg Chebbi: sunrise over the sand and nights in a bivouac.

Photo : Pierre André Leclercq, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ocean6

Essaouira

A fortified town facing the Atlantic, known for its harbour, its ramparts and its wind. Medina listed by UNESCO since 2001.

Photo : Kayaky, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage7

Aït Benhaddou

A fortified earthen village on the old caravan route. Listed by UNESCO since 1987, it has served as a setting for many films.

Photo : Petar Milošević, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital8

Rabat

The capital, on the ocean's edge: the Hassan Tower, an unfinished 12th-century minaret, and the Kasbah of the Udayas.

Photo : OHamama, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
