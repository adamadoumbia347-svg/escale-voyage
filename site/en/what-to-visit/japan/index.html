What to visit in Japan 🇯🇵

Neon lights and temples, free-roaming deer and shrines on the water: from Tokyo to Hiroshima. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

TokyoAsakusaMount FujiKyotoKinkaku-jiOsakaNaraMiyajima
Capital1

Tokyo

In Shibuya, the most famous pedestrian crossing in the world: everyone crosses at the same time.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Temple2

Asakusa

Sensō-ji, the oldest temple in Tokyo, and its large red lantern.

Photo : Tak1701d, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain3

Mount Fuji

3,776 m, the highest peak in Japan, listed by UNESCO since 2013. Seen here from the Chūreitō Pagoda.

Photo : Dang Son, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Kyoto

The former imperial capital. At the Fushimi Inari shrine, thousands of red gates form a tunnel on the hillside.

Photo : Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Temple5

Kinkaku-ji

In Kyoto, the Golden Pavilion: its floors covered in gold leaf are reflected in the pond.

Photo : Jaycangel, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

City6

Osaka

The main city of Kansai, renowned for its street food, and its castle surrounded by moats.

Photo : 663highland, CC BY 2.5, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town7

Nara

Deer roaming free in the park, and Tōdai-ji, which houses a giant bronze Buddha.

Photo : Zairon, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Shrine8

Miyajima

Near Hiroshima, the great red gate of the Itsukushima shrine seems to float at high tide. Listed by UNESCO since 1996.

Photo : JordyMeow, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
