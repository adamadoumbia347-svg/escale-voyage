What to visit in the United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪

Dubai and its towers, Abu Dhabi and its grand mosque, and the desert just outside the city. 7 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Tim Reckmann, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

City1

Dubai

The Burj Khalifa, 828 m, the tallest tower in the world, inaugurated in 2010.

Photo : Tim Reckmann, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Burj Al Arab

In Dubai, a sail-shaped hotel, built on an artificial island.

Photo : Yacine Hary, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

District3

Dubai Marina

A district of skyscrapers built around an artificial canal.

Photo : Norlando Pobre, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Island4

Palm Jumeirah

An artificial island in the shape of a palm tree, visible from the sky.

Photo : giggel, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature5

Desert

Just outside the city, dunes of orange sand as far as the eye can see.

Photo : Gfilip, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital6

Abu Dhabi

The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, in white marble, opened in 2007. Pictured here, the avenue of palm trees leading to it.

Photo : Guilhem Vellut, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Museum7

Louvre Abu Dhabi

A museum opened in 2017 beneath a vast latticed dome, at the water's edge.

Photo : Francesco Bini, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
