What to visit in Ghana 🇬🇭

A capital facing the ocean, forts steeped in history, a forest you cross on suspension bridges. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Kwaku Berko, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

AccraKwame Nkrumah MausoleumCape CoastElminaKakum National ParkKumasiMole National ParkWli Waterfalls
Capital1

Accra

Independence Square and its arch topped with the black star, symbol of the country, independent since 1957.

Photo : Kwaku Berko, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Memorial2

Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum

In Accra, the memorial to Ghana's first president, a figure of Pan-Africanism.

Photo : Fquasie, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

History3

Cape Coast

A fort on the coast, a place of remembrance of the Atlantic slave trade. Ghana's forts and castles have been listed by UNESCO since 1979.

Photo : Rjruiziii, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

History4

Elmina

Elmina Castle, founded by the Portuguese in 1482: one of the oldest European buildings south of the Sahara.

Photo : Damien Halleux Radermecker, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature5

Kakum National Park

A tropical forest that you discover from above, on a suspended walkway between the trees.

Photo : Ibnali1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Culture6

Kumasi

The capital of the Ashanti kingdom: the Manhyia Palace and one of the largest markets in West Africa.

Photo : Afus199620, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Safari7

Mole National Park

In the north, the country's largest reserve, known for its elephants.

Photo : Matti Blume, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature8

Wli Waterfalls

In the Volta Region, near the border with Togo: the highest waterfalls in the country.

Photo : Jdjdiwla, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
