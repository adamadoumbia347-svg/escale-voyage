What to visit in Egypt 🇪🇬

The pyramids, the temples of the Nile and the Red Sea: several thousand years of history along a river. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Ricardo Liberato, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Pyramids of GizaThe SphinxCairoLuxorAbu SimbelAswanAlexandriaRed Sea
Ancient site1

Pyramids of Giza

On the edge of Cairo, the three great pyramids. The pyramid of Khufu is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World still standing.

Photo : Ricardo Liberato, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site2

The Sphinx

A lion with a human head carved out of the rock, at the foot of the pyramids.

Photo : Hamerani, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital3

Cairo

A vast capital on the banks of the Nile. Historic Cairo has been listed by UNESCO since 1979.

Photo : Ghaly, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site4

Luxor

Ancient Thebes: the temple of Karnak, its avenue of ram-headed sphinxes and its hall of giant columns, on the east bank of the Nile.

Photo : Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site5

Abu Simbel

Two temples cut into the rock under Ramesses II, moved in the 1960s to save them from the waters of Lake Nasser.

Photo : Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nile6

Aswan

The main city of the south, on the banks of the Nile, and the temple of Philae, set on an island.

Photo : Mmelouk, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City7

Alexandria

The great city of the Mediterranean and the Citadel of Qaitbay, built on the site of the ancient lighthouse.

Photo : لا روسا, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach8

Red Sea

Around Hurghada: beaches, clear water and a seabed renowned for diving.

Photo : Hassan Gaafar, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
