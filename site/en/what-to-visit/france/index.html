What to visit in France 🇫🇷

Paris, but also an abbey on a rock, the French Riviera, the highest peak in the Alps and major cities of the South. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Rijin S, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

ParisVersaillesMont-Saint-MichelLyonMarseilleNiceBordeauxChamonix
Capital1

Paris

The Eiffel Tower, built for the 1889 World's Fair. The banks of the Seine have been listed by UNESCO since 1991.

Photo : Rijin S, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Palace2

Versailles

The palace of Louis XIV, its Hall of Mirrors and its gardens. Listed by UNESCO since 1979.

Photo : Dennis G. Jarvis, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage3

Mont-Saint-Michel

An abbey on a rocky islet, surrounded by the sea at high tide. Listed by UNESCO since 1979.

Photo : Eric Kilby, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Lyon

Between the Rhône and the Saône, Vieux-Lyon and Fourvière hill. Historic site listed by UNESCO since 1998.

Photo : Herbert Frank, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

City5

Marseille

The Old Port, seen here from the basilica of Notre-Dame de la Garde, on the hill.

Photo : Zairon, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside6

Nice

The Promenade des Anglais along the Baie des Anges. Listed by UNESCO since 2021.

Photo : Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

City7

Bordeaux

The Place de la Bourse and its water mirror. The Port of the Moon has been listed by UNESCO since 2007.

Photo : Gzen92, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain8

Chamonix

At the foot of Mont Blanc, the highest peak in the Alps, at more than 4,800 m. Pictured here, the Dôme du Goûter, on the route to the summit.

Photo : Alexandre Buisse, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
