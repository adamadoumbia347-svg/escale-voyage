What to visit in Senegal 🇸🇳

The land of Teranga: a capital on the ocean, islands steeped in history, a pink lake and deltas where you get around by pirogue. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Jeff Attaway, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

DakarGorée IslandLake Retba (Pink Lake)Saint-LouisSine SaloumCap SkirringToubaSaly
Capital1

Dakar

Set on a peninsula, the capital looks out over the Atlantic. The African Renaissance Monument, inaugurated in 2010, towers over the city.

Photo : Jeff Attaway, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

History2

Gorée Island

A few minutes by boat from Dakar, a car-free island of colourful houses, a place of remembrance of the slave trade. Listed by UNESCO since 1978.

Photo : Fawaz.tairou, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature3

Lake Retba (Pink Lake)

Lake Retba, north-east of Dakar, takes on pink hues depending on the season and the light. Salt is harvested there by hand.

Photo : Dr. Alexey Yakovlev, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town4

Saint-Louis

The former capital, on an island in the Senegal River, linked by the Faidherbe Bridge. Listed by UNESCO since 2000.

Photo : Issathiome, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature5

Sine Saloum

A delta of mangroves, islands and sea inlets that you explore by pirogue. Listed by UNESCO since 2011.

Photo : Issiaga0, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach6

Cap Skirring

In Casamance, in the south of the country: long beaches of pale sand lined with palm trees.

Photo : XDurang, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Holy city7

Touba

The holy city of Mouridism and its Great Mosque, one of the largest in West Africa.

Photo : Issathiome, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach8

Saly

On the Petite Côte, south of Dakar: the country's best-known seaside resort.

Photo : Davric, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
