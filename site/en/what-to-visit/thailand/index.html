What to visit in Thailand 🇹🇭

Golden temples, former capitals in ruins and islands with limestone cliffs. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

BangkokGrand PalaceAyutthayaChiang MaiChiang RaiPhuketPhi Phi IslandsKrabi
Capital1

Bangkok

On the banks of the Chao Phraya River, Wat Arun, the Temple of Dawn, and its tower covered in porcelain.

Photo : Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Grand Palace

In Bangkok, the former palace of the kings, founded in 1782, and the Temple of the Emerald Buddha.

Photo : Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

History3

Ayutthaya

The former capital of the Kingdom of Siam, north of Bangkok: ruined temples listed by UNESCO since 1991.

Photo : Evilarry, Public domain, Wikimedia Commons

City4

Chiang Mai

The main city of the north, surrounded by mountains. The golden temple of Doi Suthep overlooks it.

Photo : เทวประภาส มากคล้าย, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Temple5

Chiang Rai

Wat Rong Khun, or the White Temple: an entirely white contemporary work, opened in 1997.

Photo : Chainwit., CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach6

Phuket

The country's largest island, in the Andaman Sea: long sandy beaches.

Photo : ADwarf, Public domain, Wikimedia Commons

Islands7

Phi Phi Islands

Limestone cliffs that plunge into turquoise water, between Phuket and Krabi.

Photo : Nnthurber, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach8

Krabi

Railay beach, surrounded by cliffs: you get there by boat.

Photo : Wendy Harman, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons
