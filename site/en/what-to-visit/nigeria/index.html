What to visit in Nigeria 🇳🇬

Lagos and its lagoon, a modern capital, great granite rocks, a sacred grove and waterfalls. 7 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Clara Sanchiz, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

LagosLekki-Ikoyi Link BridgeAbujaOlumo RockOsun-Osogbo Sacred GroveErin-Ijesha WaterfallsIdanre Hills
City1

Lagos

The country's largest city, built on islands and around a lagoon.

Photo : Clara Sanchiz, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge

In Lagos, a cable-stayed bridge opened in 2013, linking Lekki to Ikoyi across the lagoon.

Photo : Chippla, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital3

Abuja

The federal capital since 1991, in the centre of the country, and its national mosque with a golden dome.

Photo : Mark Fischer, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery4

Olumo Rock

In Abeokuta, a granite rock that served as a refuge for the Egba people in the 19th century. You climb to the top for the view over the city.

Photo : 'Yemi King, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Heritage5

Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove

A sacred Yoruba forest along the Osun River, with its shrines and sculptures. Listed by UNESCO since 2005.

Photo : ExploreWithTunde, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Waterfall6

Erin-Ijesha Waterfalls

In Osun State, a waterfall on several levels, in the middle of the forest.

Photo : Coolambrose, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery7

Idanre Hills

In the south-west, great granite domes surrounding the town of Idanre.

Photo : Sasu photography, CC0, Wikimedia Commons
