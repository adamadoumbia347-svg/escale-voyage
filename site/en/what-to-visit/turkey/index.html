What to visit in Turkey 🇹🇷

Istanbul between two continents, the fairy chimneys of Cappadocia, ancient ruins and the Turquoise Coast. 8 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : Arild Vågen, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

IstanbulBlue MosqueBosphorusCappadociaPamukkaleEphesusAntalyaBodrum
City1

Istanbul

Hagia Sophia, built in the 6th century. The historic areas have been listed by UNESCO since 1985.

Photo : Arild Vågen, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

Blue Mosque

In Istanbul, the Sultan Ahmed Mosque, completed in the early 17th century, and its six minarets.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery3

Bosphorus

The strait that separates Europe from Asia, in the heart of Istanbul. Pictured here, the Ortaköy Mosque, at the foot of the bridge.

Photo : Darwinek, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Scenery4

Cappadocia

Fairy chimneys and villages carved into the rock, which you fly over by hot-air balloon. Listed by UNESCO since 1985.

Photo : MusikAnimal, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature5

Pamukkale

The "cotton castle": white terraced pools, formed by hot springs. Listed by UNESCO since 1988.

Photo : Slyronit, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Ancient site6

Ephesus

A great ancient city and the façade of the Library of Celsus. Listed by UNESCO since 2015.

Photo : Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside7

Antalya

On the Mediterranean, an old town around a small harbour, at the foot of the mountains.

Photo : REHBER0770, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Seaside8

Bodrum

On the Aegean Sea, a harbour overlooked by the Castle of St Peter, built in the 15th century.

Photo : Mustang Joe, CC0, Wikimedia Commons
