What to visit in Côte d'Ivoire 🇨🇮

An economic capital on the edge of the lagoon, the largest basilica in the world, listed colonial towns and beaches lined with coconut palms. 9 cities and places to see, in photos.

Photo : RyansWorld, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

AbidjanSt Paul's CathedralYamoussoukroGrand-BassamAssinieManSan-PédroSassandraTaï National Park
City1

Abidjan

The economic capital stretches around the Ébrié Lagoon. The Plateau district lines up its towers along the water, facing Cocody and Treichville.

Photo : RyansWorld, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

Landmark2

St Paul's Cathedral

In Plateau, a cathedral of bold architecture, consecrated in 1985, with its large stained-glass windows and its view over the lagoon.

Photo : Edison McCullen, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Capital3

Yamoussoukro

The political capital is home to the Basilica of Our Lady of Peace, consecrated in 1990 and often described as the largest church in the world.

Photo : Didierwiki, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Historic town4

Grand-Bassam

The former colonial capital, east of Abidjan: old houses, craftspeople and a beach. Pictured here, the former governor's palace, now the National Costume Museum. Its historic town has been listed by UNESCO since 2012.

Photo : Modjou, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach5

Assinie

A strip of sand between the ocean and the lagoon, in the east of the country: coconut palms, pirogues and weekends by the water.

Photo : Dadrik, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Mountain6

Man

In the west, the "city of 18 mountains": waterfalls, forest, liana bridges and the outline of the Dent de Man.

Photo : Sahi Tia, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach7

San-Pédro

A major port in the south-west and, around it, long wild beaches facing the Atlantic.

Photo : Dadrik, CC0, Wikimedia Commons

Beach8

Sassandra

A small fishing town at the mouth of the river of the same name: coconut palms, pirogues and quiet beaches.

Photo : Mégougou, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons

Nature9

Taï National Park

In the south-west, one of the last large primary forests in West Africa, a refuge for chimpanzees. Listed by UNESCO since 1982.

Photo : Alexey Yakovlev, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons
