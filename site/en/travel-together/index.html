Travelling together: who pays the least?

One destination, your dates, and the round-trip fare found from each major departure city, cheapest first, with the total for the group.

Lowest round-trip fare found recently by travellers on Aviasales (via Travelpayouts), per person, for the month of the chosen dates. The exact fare depends on dates and is confirmed on the booking site.

What it is for

  • A family spread between Paris, Brussels and Milan meeting in Abidjan for the holidays: everyone sees at a glance what their ticket costs, and who should fly from another city.
  • Friends hesitating between Marseille and Paris: the gap can exceed €150 per person on some dates.
  • Someone joining relatives: if the flight from their city is much dearer, a train or bus to another airport can be worth it.

Three tips

  • Compare the total cost: ticket + trip to the airport + a possible hotel night. A cheaper flight 400 km away is not always cheaper.
  • For a group, book early: the fares shown are often the last seats at that price, and it rises as soon as a few seats go.
  • Travelling with children? Check the airline's baggage allowance first: baggage by airline.
