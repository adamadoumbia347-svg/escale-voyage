    Frequently asked questions

    When is Tabaski 2027?

    Tabaski 2027 is expected around Sunday 16 or Monday 17 May 2027. The official date is set in each country by the religious authorities after the moon sighting, so it can differ by a day from one country to another (Mali, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea, Niger…). Check your country's official announcement before fixing your dates.

    Are Tabaski and Eid al-Adha the same celebration?

    Yes. Tabaski is the name used in West Africa for Eid al-Adha, the Feast of the Sacrifice.

    Why does the date of Tabaski change every year?

    The celebration follows the Islamic lunar calendar, so it moves about ten to eleven days earlier each year in the civil calendar.

    When should I buy my flight for Tabaski?

    As early as you can: three to four months ahead if possible. Six to eight weeks before the celebration, fares are usually already higher, because many families travel on the same dates.

    Which day is cheapest to fly?

    Avoid the two days just before the celebration: they are the busiest. Leaving a few days earlier and coming back a few days later is often cheaper. The table on this page shows the cheapest day found.

    Should I plan for an extra bag?

    People often travel with gifts. Check the allowance of your fare and add the bag when you book: it costs less than at the airport. The exact rule depends on the airline.
