Staying in Kigali: what to know

Kigali is a compact city of hills. Visitors stay in the centre (Kiyovu) for business, in Kimihurura or Kacyiru for restaurants and embassies, or in Remera near the airport. The airport is about ten kilometres from the centre.

  • The city is hilly: check the map before choosing a hotel if you plan to walk.
  • Journeys between districts are short: there is no need to pay more to be central.
  • For a night arrival, ask your hotel to arrange the transfer.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Kigali airport is about ten kilometres from the centre: journeys are short whichever area you choose.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Where to stay in Kigali?

Kiyovu, in the centre, for business; Kimihurura and Kacyiru for restaurants and embassies; Remera to be close to the airport.

How far is Kigali airport from the city?

About ten kilometres from the centre: the journey is short.

Is an apartment a better choice?

For a stay of a week or more, a furnished apartment with a kitchen often costs less per night than a hotel, especially for a family.

Which area should I stay in, in Kigali?

Kiyovu: city centre, business district and hotels ; Kimihurura and Kacyiru: restaurants, embassies, convention centre ; Remera: lively, close to the airport and the stadium.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Kigali?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
