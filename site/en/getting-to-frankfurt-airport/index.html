Getting to Frankfurt airport (FRA)

Train, metro, bus, car: how to reach Frankfurt before your flight, and what to check the day before.

By public transport

  • S-Bahn S8 and S9 from the main station (Hauptbahnhof) to the airport regional station, at terminal 1.
  • Long-distance trains (ICE): “Frankfurt Flughafen Fernbahnhof” station has direct trains from many German cities.

Do not confuse it with Frankfurt-Hahn (HHN), an airport very far from the city.

Timetables, fares and engineering works: the airport's official site.

By car

  • Official car parks can be booked online on the airport's site: it is usually cheaper than turning up without a booking.
  • To drop someone off, follow the “drop-off” signs: stopping there is limited to a few minutes.
  • Allow a margin for traffic at rush hour and for the shuttle between the car park and the terminal.

The day before

  • Check the terminal and the check-in deadline on your ticket.
  • See whether there is a strike or engineering work on your line, and have a plan B.
  • Weigh your bags: each airline's allowance.
  • Go through the pre-flight check: passport, visa, documents.

Frequently asked questions

How long before the flight should I arrive at Frankfurt?

For a long-haul flight to Africa, airlines often recommend arriving about three hours before departure. Your airline's check-in deadline is shown on the ticket: that is the one that counts.

Train or car?

The train or metro avoids traffic jams and the cost of parking for a long stay. The car is still practical for a family with many suitcases or for a very early flight: in that case, book the official car park online in advance.

Where do I find timetables and fares?

On the airport's official site and on the transport operator's site. Escale shows neither timetables nor fares, because they change: check the day before departure, especially if there is a strike or engineering work.
