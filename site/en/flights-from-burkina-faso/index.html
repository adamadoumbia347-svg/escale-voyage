Our tips for flying from Burkina Faso

  • For the UK, the Schengen area, the US and Canada, apply for your visa early: appointments are often booked more than a month ahead before summer and Christmas.
  • Compare flights with a stop in Istanbul, Addis Ababa, Casablanca or Dubai: they are often much cheaper than a direct flight.
  • Check the baggage allowance before comparing two fares: for a long stay, the second suitcase changes everything.
  • Our partners find prices in euros; the CFA franc equivalent is a guide and the final fare is shown on the booking site.
